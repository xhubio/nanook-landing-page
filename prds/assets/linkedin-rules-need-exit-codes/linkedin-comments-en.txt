What the sense check looks like: every QA walkthrough asks five questions: the job, can it be played through, work saved, connection to the process, honest maturity. The agent does not fix these findings by itself. We sort them together: fix, rework, remove from the navigation, or delete.

Thank you for reading along through nine parts. What this setup builds is in real use, for example https://invoice-api.xhub.io and https://verein.xhub.io. The whole report as one page: https://nanook.xhub.io/articles/agentic-software-development
