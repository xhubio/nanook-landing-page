=== Comment 1 — Torsten Link (author; first comment under the post, within the first hour) ===

People always ask: what is actually built in these repositories? The multi-product SaaS in the post is a set of real products on shared modules, for example the e-invoicing API (https://invoice-api.xhub.io), the software for clubs and associations (https://verein.xhub.io) and the software for trades businesses (https://dashandwerk.xhub.io). That is why requirements live per product: the unit that is designed and released together decides the folder.

And one rule that saved a lot of time: a blocker leaves the plan and gets a folder of its own. But check it first. Once, "no access" was in fact a self-service step that nobody had tried.
