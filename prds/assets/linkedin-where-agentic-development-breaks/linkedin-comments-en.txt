=== Comment 1 — Torsten Link (author; first comment under the post, within the first hour) ===

A flip side that is not in the post. After the incident with the 12/12 green tests came the rule: known defects are always red, with a message. Two days later, three test stands had a red marker for an object that no longer existed. Even a good rule needs someone who checks that its subject still exists.

And about absences: a walkthrough with the owner account never finds a missing permission check. The menu entry had it, the route did not. A guard test found it in seconds.

One of the five cases of "the measuring instrument lies" is about us: the table engine that reports errors only in its logger is Nanook. A broken sheet then looks like a good one.
